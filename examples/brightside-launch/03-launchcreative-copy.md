## Landing Page Copy (outline, per the LP framework)

1. **Hero** — H: "Mornings without the jolt." Sub: "Brightside wakes you with a
   30-minute sunrise and winds you down with a sunset." CTA: "Start your 30-night
   trial." Visual: hand reaching to a glowing bedside Brightside, pre-dawn room.
2. **Problem** — the phone-alarm jolt, the groggy first hour, the snooze spiral.
3. **Solution** — introduce Brightside + positioning statement.
4. **How it works** — Sunrise (wake) → Sunset (wind-down) → Simple (no app), each
   one line + visual.
5. **Proof** — beta-tester quotes ("I stopped using my phone as an alarm after
   night two"), 30-night-trial badge.
6. **Offer** — $99, free shipping, 30-night money-back, what's in the box.
7. **FAQ** — "Is it just a lamp?" "Do I need an app?" "Will it wake a heavy
   sleeper?" "Return policy?"
8. **Final CTA** — repeat hero CTA.

## Creative Brief (for the 40s demo recut)

```
Platform: Meta / YouTube   Format: 40s video (vertical + 16:9)
Hook (0–3s): dark room, phone alarm BUZZ — then cut to silence as the Brightside
  glow begins. Pattern interrupt.
Main message: waking to light feels completely different from waking to a siren.
Tone: warm, calm, cinematic — not clinical, not gadgety.
Visual direction: real bedroom, real person, golden light bloom; show sunset
  wind-down at night too. No on-product text (sound-off safe captions instead).
CTA: "Wake up on the bright side — 30-night trial."
Avoid: medical claims, lab/clinical look, feature-spec overload.
```
