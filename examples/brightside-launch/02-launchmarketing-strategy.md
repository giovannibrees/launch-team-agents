## ICP Summary

The buyer is 28–45, wakes earlier than they'd like, and starts most days groggy
and behind. They're a light sleeper who already spends on sleep and wellness
(supplements, a good mattress, a meditation app) and resents that a screaming
phone alarm is the first thing that happens to them each day. They believe small
environmental changes compound into better days, and they're willing to pay a
premium for something that *feels* considered rather than gadgety. They discover
products on Instagram and YouTube reviews, research before buying $100 items, and
their trigger is the stretch of darker autumn mornings when getting up gets
visibly harder.

They are **Problem-Aware / Solution-Aware** (see `ad-creative-playbook.md`): they
know mornings are rough and that sunrise clocks exist — they're not yet sold that
*this* one is worth it over Philips or Hatch.

## Positioning Statement

> For people who wake up groggy and hate their phone alarm, Brightside is the
> wake-up light that makes mornings feel gentle instead of jarring — unlike a
> generic smart lamp, because it pairs a gradual sunrise with a true sunset
> wind-down, validated by 30 beta sleepers.

## Top 3 Angles

1. **"The opposite of a phone alarm"** — pattern interrupt against the universal
   jolt of a phone alarm. Works because it names a pain everyone feels daily.
2. **"Wake up on the bright side"** — identity/feeling angle: the kind of morning
   person you wish you were. Leans into aspiration, not features.
3. **"Sunrise *and* sunset"** — the differentiator vs. competitors who emphasize
   only the wake side. The wind-down is the under-told half of the story.

> ⚠️ Compliance note for launchcreative: keep this **wellness, not medical**. No
> claims about treating SAD, depression, or insomnia. (`ad-policy-compliance.md`)

## Channel Recommendation

Consumer-gadget split, validate Meta + Google first (TikTok pending account):

- **Primary — Meta:** 55% (~$11,000) — best for cold visual-product acquisition
  and retargeting the existing pixel/IG audience.
- **Secondary — Google:** 25% (~$5,000) — capture existing demand: brand +
  "sunrise alarm clock / wake up light" category + competitor comparison.
- **Test — TikTok:** 15% (~$3,000) — start once account + pixel are live;
  demos well in video. **Hold until ready.**
- **Support — YouTube:** 5% (~$1,000) — the 40s demo against sleep/morning-routine
  content.

Target blended **3.0x ROAS**; floor **2.5x** before cutting (margin 65%).

## Funnel Map

```
Cold ad (Meta/Google)
   → Sunrise landing page (problem → sunrise+sunset mechanism → proof → offer)
   → Add to cart ($99, free shipping, 30-night guarantee)
   → Checkout
   → Post-purchase email (setup tips → review request → accessories)
Retargeting: 7/14/30-day visitors + IG engagers → offer-led ads
```

## Competitive Gaps

- Competitors sell the **sunrise**; almost nobody sells the **sunset wind-down**
  with equal weight. Own the "both ends of the day" position.
- Competitor ads read clinical or gadgety. A warm, "feeling of the morning"
  treatment is open territory.
- Their 1–2★ reviews complain about fiddly apps → lean into "simple, no app
  required."

## Risks

- New brand = no purchase pixel history; Meta will take ~500 events to optimize.
  Budget for a learning period; don't judge week 1 on ROAS alone.
- Price objection vs. a basic lamp → the LP must build the mechanism and proof
  before showing price.
- TikTok delay means the 15% is idle until setup; reallocate to Meta if TikTok
  isn't live by week 2.
- Seasonal tailwind is still a few weeks early — expect efficiency to *improve*
  into autumn.
