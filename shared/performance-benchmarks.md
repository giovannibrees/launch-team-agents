# Performance Benchmarks

Reference benchmarks for paid advertising across product categories. Use these to set expectations before a launch and to evaluate performance during one. These are real-world ranges, not platform-published ideals.

---

## Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

### Cold Traffic Benchmarks (Prospecting Campaigns)

| Metric | Poor | Acceptable | Good | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTR (link click) | Under 0.8% | 0.8-1.2% | 1.2-2.0% | Above 2.0% |
| CPC | Above $2.50 | $1.50-$2.50 | $0.80-$1.50 | Under $0.80 |
| CPM | Above $25 | $15-$25 | $8-$15 | Under $8 |
| Landing page CVR | Under 1% | 1-2% | 2-4% | Above 4% |
| Hook rate (3s video views / impressions) | Under 20% | 20-30% | 30-45% | Above 45% |
| Video completion (15-30s video) | Under 15% | 15-25% | 25-40% | Above 40% |

## Google Ads

### Search Campaign Benchmarks

| Metric | Poor | Acceptable | Good | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTR (branded terms) | Under 5% | 5-10% | 10-20% | Above 20% |
| CTR (non-branded) | Under 2% | 2-4% | 4-8% | Above 8% |
| Quality Score | 1-3 | 4-6 | 7-8 | 9-10 |
| Conversion rate | Under 2% | 2-4% | 4-8% | Above 8% |
| Cost per conversion | Varies by category — compare to product margin | | | |

### Quality Score Impact on CPC

Quality Score directly affects how much you pay per click:
- Score 10: ~50% CPC discount vs. average
- Score 7-9: Slight discount
- Score 5-6: Pays roughly average auction price
- Score 3-4: Pays 25-50% premium
- Score 1-2: Pays significant premium or rarely wins auctions

Improve Quality Score by: improving ad relevance (match headline to keyword), improving LP relevance (match LP content to keyword), improving expected CTR (test better ad copy).

### Performance Max Benchmarks

PMax benchmarks vary significantly by industry. Watch:
- Asset group performance (which combinations Google is serving)
- Search themes driving conversions (check Insights tab)
- ROAS vs. target (set target CPA/ROAS and let it optimize for 2-3 weeks before judging)

## TikTok Ads

### Cold Traffic Benchmarks

| Metric | Poor | Acceptable | Good | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CPM | Above $12 | $7-$12 | $4-$7 | Under $4 |
| CTR | Under 0.5% | 0.5-1.0% | 1.0-1.8% | Above 1.8% |
| Video completion (15s) | Under 20% | 20-30% | 30-45% | Above 45% |
| CPC | Above $1.50 | $0.80-$1.50 | $0.40-$0.80 | Under $0.40 |
| CVR (LP) | Under 1% | 1-2% | 2-4% | Above 4% |

### TikTok vs. Meta: When TikTok Wins

TikTok typically outperforms Meta when:
- Target audience is under 35
- Product demos well in video (satisfying, visual, fast)
- Creative is native-feeling (UGC-style, not polished production)
- Product has inherent virality (before/after, surprising result, satisfying process)

TikTok typically underperforms Meta when:
- Target audience is over 45
- Product requires explanation (complex B2B, nuanced benefits)
- You cannot produce consistent new video creative (TikTok fatigues faster)
- Conversion tracking is not reliable (TikTok's pixel is less mature)

## Email Marketing Benchmarks (Launch Sequences)

| Metric | Poor | Acceptable | Good | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open rate (pre-launch) | Under 20% | 20-30% | 30-45% | Above 45% |
| Open rate (post-launch) | Under 15% | 15-25% | 25-35% | Above 35% |
| Click rate | Under 1.5% | 1.5-3% | 3-6% | Above 6% |
| Unsubscribe rate | Above 0.5% | 0.2-0.5% | Under 0.2% | Under 0.1% |

Email converts higher than paid ads for warm audiences. A 10,000-person email list is worth more than $10,000 in cold ad spend in most launch scenarios. Build the list before the launch.

## Conversion Funnel Benchmarks

Typical funnel drop-off for a product launch:

```
Ad impression         100%
Ad click              1-3% (CTR)
Landing page view     70-85% (of clicks; some bounce before loading)
Engaged (3s+)         40-60%
Add to cart           5-15%
Checkout initiated    50-70% of add to carts
Purchase complete     50-70% of checkouts initiated
```

Overall: expect 0.5-2.5% of all LP visitors to purchase on first visit, depending on product, price, and audience warmth.

**Where most launches lose money:**

1. **Ad to LP click:** Ad does not match LP — set false expectations
2. **LP to add to cart:** LP does not build enough trust or desire before showing price
3. **Checkout abandonment:** Unexpected costs (shipping), friction in checkout, lack of trust signals
4. **Ad spend on wrong audiences:** Traffic volume but zero purchase intent

## Budget Pacing Reference

For a 30-day launch with a fixed budget:

| Phase | Days | % of Budget | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Testing | 1-7 | 20-25% | Test audiences and creatives at low spend; identify winners |
| Validation | 8-14 | 25-30% | Scale winning combos; continue testing challengers |
| Scale | 15-25 | 35-40% | Full spend on winners; maintain creative freshness |
| Wind-down | 26-30 | 10-15% | Reduce spend; focus retargeting; capture stragglers |

Never spend 100% of budget equally across 30 days. Frontload testing, backload scale (but only after you have validated what works).
