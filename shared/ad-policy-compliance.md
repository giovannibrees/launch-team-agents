# Ad Policy & Compliance Guide

Ad disapprovals are one of the most common ways a launch loses days it can't
afford. Most rejections are avoidable — they come from a handful of repeat
offenders. This guide is the pre-flight check for `launchcreative` (who writes
the copy) and `launchads` (who submits it). When a platform does reject an ad,
it routes through `launchteam` per `human-approval-policy.md`.

> This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Platform policies change —
> link out to the live policy centers (below) and verify restricted categories
> for your specific product and region before launch.

---

## The universal rules (all platforms)

These cause the majority of disapprovals:

- **No unsupported or absolute claims.** "Cure," "guaranteed," "100% effective,"
  "lose 20 lbs in a week." If you can't prove it, don't claim it. Soften to
  what's true and cite the evidence.
- **No "personal attributes" callouts.** Don't imply you know who the viewer
  *is* or what they suffer from. ❌ "Struggling with insomnia?" / "As a
  diabetic, you…" ✅ "Mornings feel rough? Here's a gentler way to wake up."
  This is the #1 Meta rejection and applies to health, financial status, age,
  religion, sexual orientation, name, etc.
- **No sensational / shocking content.** Before/after extremes, zoomed body
  parts, "one weird trick," fear-baiting.
- **Landing page must match the ad** and be functional: same offer, no
  pop-up-only exits, no broken links, working back button, accessible, and free
  of prohibited content. Platforms review the LP, not just the ad.
- **No misleading mechanics** — fake "X" close buttons, fake play buttons, fake
  UI, fake countdowns that reset.
- **Disclose materially.** Endorsements/testimonials should be genuine and, where
  required, disclosed.

## Restricted categories (need extra care / certification)

If the product touches any of these, check the platform's specific policy and
budget extra time for review/approval:

- **Health & wellness** — supplements, weight loss, "medical" devices. Health
  claims and before/after imagery are heavily restricted (esp. Meta).
- **Financial / crypto / "make money"** — often require certification and carry
  strict claim rules.
- **CBD / hemp / alcohol / tobacco & vaping** — banned or geo/age-gated.
- **Housing, employment, credit** (Meta **Special Ad Categories**) — restrict
  targeting (no age/gender/zip targeting). Must self-declare.
- **Healthcare, gambling, pharma** (Google restricted) — certification/geo gated.
- **Politics / social issues** — authorization and disclaimers required.

## Platform-specific landmines

### Meta (Facebook + Instagram)
- Personal attributes (above) — the big one.
- Health: no before/after, no implied negative self-perception, no "ideal body."
- Special Ad Categories self-declaration for housing/employment/credit.
- Too much text on images is no longer a hard reject but still hurts delivery.
- Repeated violations → **account/ad-account restriction.** Treat the first
  disapproval as a signal, not a nuisance.

### Google Ads
- **No competitor trademarks in ad text** (you may bid on the keyword, but the
  ad copy can't use their mark).
- Misrepresentation: no fake discounts, no "free" that isn't, clear pricing.
- Healthcare/financial restrictions; some require certification.
- Destination/LP requirements: original content, working, clear contact/refund
  info for ecommerce.
- Malicious/unwanted software and "unacceptable business practices" → harsh.

### TikTok
- Native-feeling, but **truthful** — exaggerated/absolute claims still rejected.
- Before/after and body-image content restricted.
- Prohibited industries list is broad; check before building.
- Landing page and pixel must be functional; misleading flows get pulled.

## Pre-submission checklist (run before any ad goes to review)

- [ ] No absolute/guaranteed/medical claims — every claim is provable.
- [ ] No "personal attributes" — copy doesn't tell the viewer who they are.
- [ ] No restricted-category issue, or certification is in hand.
- [ ] No competitor trademark in ad copy (Google).
- [ ] Special Ad Category declared if housing/employment/credit (Meta).
- [ ] Landing page matches the ad, loads, and is free of prohibited content.
- [ ] No fake UI, countdowns, or "free" that isn't.
- [ ] Testimonials are genuine; claims in them are also provable.
- [ ] Images: no shocking/sensational content; before/after only where allowed.

---

## When an ad is disapproved

1. **Read the exact policy cited** — the rejection names it.
2. **Fix the specific element** (usually a claim or an image), don't resubmit
   blind — repeated rejections hurt account health.
3. **Request review** once corrected.
4. **Escalate to `launchteam`** with the rejection reason if it blocks go-live or
   recurs. Account restriction is an immediate FLAG (see
   `agent-communication-protocol.md`).
