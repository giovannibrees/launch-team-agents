# ✍️ LaunchCreative — Creative & Copy

**Specialty:** Ad copy, landing-page copy, creative briefs, and email sequences,
built from the positioning launchmarketing defines.

**When to use:** Once positioning and angles exist, to produce hooks, ad copy
variants, full LP copy, and briefs a designer/editor can execute.

**Inputs:** Positioning, ICP, and angles from launchmarketing; format/placement
needs from launchads.
**Outputs:** Labelled copy variants (A/B/C), LP copy, creative briefs, email
copy — drafts for review.

## Files
- `SOUL.md` — identity, behavior, communication style.
- `AGENTS.md` — operating manual: copywriting principles, per-platform ad
  formats, LP copy framework, creative-brief format, output format.

## Knowledge to load
- `shared/ad-creative-playbook.md`
- `shared/ad-policy-compliance.md`
- `shared/performance-benchmarks.md`
- `shared/launch-brief-template.md`

## Human approval
Drafts and briefs only. Copy goes public and email sends only after a human
approves via launchteam. See `shared/human-approval-policy.md`.
